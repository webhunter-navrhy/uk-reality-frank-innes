Description

An exceptional opportunity to acquire this beautifully renovated four-bedroom semi-detached family home, ideally positioned in the heart of Chesterfield. Offering spacious and versatile accommodation across three floors, a large rear garden, off-street parking and a garage, this stunning property has been thoughtfully modernised throughout and is perfectly suited to growing families seeking a home ready to move straight into.

The current owners have invested significant time and effort into transforming the property, creating a stylish and contemporary home finished to an excellent standard throughout. The result is a property that seamlessly combines modern family living with generous and practical accommodation.

The ground floor features a welcoming entrance hall, a spacious living room providing the perfect environment for relaxing with family, and an impressive open-plan kitchen/dining room. Designed with modern lifestyles in mind, this sociable space offers ample room for dining, entertaining and everyday family life, making it the true heart of the home. A convenient ground-floor WC and integral garage further enhance the practicality of the accommodation.

To the first floor are two well-proportioned double bedrooms, including a superb principal bedroom benefiting from its own en-suite shower room, together with a modern family bathroom. The second floor offers two further generously sized bedrooms and an additional shower room, making this an ideal layout for larger families, visiting guests or those working from home.

Externally, the property continues to impress. To the front, there is off-street parking and access to the garage, whilst the substantial rear garden provides an excellent outdoor space for families and keen gardeners alike. Whether enjoying summer entertaining, children's play areas or creating your own garden sanctuary, this wonderful space offers endless possibilities.

Situated within close proximity to Chesterfield town centre, residents can enjoy easy access to an excellent range of shops, restaurants, bars and leisure facilities. Commuters will particularly appreciate the property's convenient location, with Chesterfield Train Station, the A617 and the M1 motorway all within easy reach, providing excellent transport links across the region.

Beautifully presented throughout and offering an abundance of living space, this outstanding home truly is the perfect family property. Early viewing is highly recommended to appreciate the quality of accommodation on offer and the superb improvements carried out by the current owners.
